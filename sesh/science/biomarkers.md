<section class="section wrap">
  <div class="idea-grid">
    <div class="card">
      <h2>What a biomarker is</h2>
      <ul>
        <li>Something in the body that can be measured and tells you about a disease.</li>
        <li>In cancer, it's often a change in the tumor's DNA or proteins.</li>
      </ul>
    </div>
    <div class="card">
      <h2>Three kinds</h2>
      <ul>
        <li>Diagnostic: is cancer there?</li>
        <li>Prognostic: how might it progress?</li>
        <li>Predictive: will this treatment work?</li>
      </ul>
    </div>
    <div class="card">
      <h2>HLA LOH</h2>
      <ul>
        <li>HLA: genes that put the "ID badges" on a cell.</li>
        <li>LOH (loss of heterozygosity): the tumor loses one of its two copies.</li>
        <li>Fewer badges can mean immune cells miss the tumor.</li>
        <li>It's a predictive biomarker for immunotherapy, and the one RELOAD looks for.</li>
      </ul>
    </div>
    <div class="card">
      <h2>Why they're hard to measure</h2>
      <ul>
        <li>Tumors are uneven, so one tissue sample can miss changes.</li>
        <li>Tumors change over time.</li>
        <li>Some changes, like HLA LOH, are hard to detect with standard tests.</li>
      </ul>
    </div>
