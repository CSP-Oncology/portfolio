<section class="section wrap">
  <div class="idea-grid">
    <div class="card">
      <h2>What cancer is</h2>
      <ul>
        <li>Cells normally grow, divide and die on schedule.</li>
        <li>Mutations in DNA can break those controls.</li>
        <li>Cells that keep dividing form a tumor and can spread.</li>
      </ul>
    </div>
    <div class="card">
      <h2>The immune system's role</h2>
      <ul>
        <li>T cells patrol the body looking for abnormal cells.</li>
        <li>Cells show bits of their proteins on HLA molecules, like an ID badge.</li>
        <li>If a badge looks wrong, the T cell attacks.</li>
      </ul>
    </div>
    <div class="card">
      <h2>How tumors hide</h2>
      <ul>
        <li>Some tumors switch off the "brakes" that let T cells attack.</li>
        <li>Others lose HLA genes, so they stop showing an ID badge. This is HLA LOH.</li>
      </ul>
    </div>
    <div class="card">
      <h2>Why it matters for treatment</h2>
      <ul>
        <li>Immunotherapy helps T cells find and attack cancer.</li>
        <li>If the tumor has hidden its badges, immunotherapy may work less well.</li>
      </ul>
    </div>
