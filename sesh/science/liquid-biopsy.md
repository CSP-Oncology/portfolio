<section class="section wrap">
  <div class="idea-grid">
    <div class="card">
      <h2>What it is</h2>
      <ul>
        <li>Tumor cells shed small pieces of DNA into the blood.</li>
        <li>A liquid biopsy collects a blood sample and reads that cell-free DNA (cfDNA).</li>
      </ul>
    </div>
    <div class="card">
      <h2>Tissue vs. liquid</h2>
      <ul>
        <li>Tissue biopsy: surgery or a needle, one spot, hard to repeat.</li>
        <li>Liquid biopsy: a blood draw, picks up DNA from the whole tumor, easy to repeat.</li>
      </ul>
    </div>
    <div class="card">
      <h2>Advantages</h2>
      <ul>
        <li>Accounts for tumor heterogeneity (differences across a tumor).</li>
        <li>Allows serial monitoring without repeated invasive procedures.</li>
        <li>Can save cost and time.</li>
      </ul>
    </div>
    <div class="card">
      <h2>How the DNA is read</h2>
      <ul>
        <li>Next-generation sequencing (NGS) reads millions of DNA pieces at once.</li>
        <li>Analysis then picks out the tumor's DNA from normal DNA.</li>
      </ul>
    </div>
