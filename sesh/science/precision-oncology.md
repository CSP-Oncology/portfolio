<section class="section wrap">
  <div class="idea-grid">
    <div class="card">
      <h2>The idea</h2>
      <ul>
        <li>Two patients with the "same" cancer can have very different tumors.</li>
        <li>Precision oncology tests each tumor and picks treatment based on what it finds.</li>
      </ul>
    </div>
    <div class="card">
      <h2>One size vs. tailored</h2>
      <ul>
        <li>Standard: treatment chosen mainly by cancer type and stage.</li>
        <li>Precision: treatment also chosen by the tumor's genes and biomarkers.</li>
      </ul>
    </div>
    <div class="card">
      <h2>Precision immuno-oncology</h2>
      <ul>
        <li>Focuses on immunotherapy.</li>
        <li>Asks: will this patient's immune system be able to see the tumor?</li>
        <li>This is the area Sesh works in.</li>
      </ul>
    </div>
    <div class="card">
      <h2>Where it's used</h2>
      <ul>
        <li>Treatment selection: choosing a therapy.</li>
        <li>Response monitoring: checking if it's working.</li>
        <li>Clinical trials: grouping patients for studies.</li>
      </ul>
    </div>
