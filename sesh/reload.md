      <h2>What it is</h2>
      <p>A liquid biopsy test designed to detect immunotherapy-associated predictive biomarkers in cancer patients, such as HLA LOH, so oncologists can adapt treatment proactively.</p>
    </div>
    <div class="card">
      <h2>Key features</h2>
      <ul class="check-list">
        <li><strong>Minimally invasive:</strong> needs only a blood sample</li>
        <li><strong>Real-time monitoring:</strong> tracks treatment response over time</li>
        <li><strong>Clinical validation:</strong> backed by scientific research</li>
      </ul>
    </div>
    <div class="card">
      <h2>How it works</h2>
      <ol class="steps">
        <li><div><strong>Blood draw</strong><span>Cell-free DNA (cfDNA) is collected from blood</span></div></li>
        <li><div><strong>Sequencing</strong><span>Next-generation sequencing reads the DNA</span></div></li>
        <li><div><strong>Report</strong><span>Results guide treatment strategy</span></div></li>
      </ol>
    </div>
    <div class="card">
      <h2>Clinical applications</h2>
      <ul class="check-list">
        <li><strong>Treatment selection:</strong> choosing therapy from biomarker profiles</li>
        <li><strong>Response monitoring:</strong> tracking treatment and tumor changes</li>
        <li><strong>Clinical trials:</strong> grouping patients and measuring endpoints</li>
      </ul>
    </div>
